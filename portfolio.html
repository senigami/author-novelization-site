Portfolio & Case Studies

Demonstrating the transformation from raw text to published product.

⚠️ Notice: All examples below are public domain or fictional demonstrations.

I protect my clients' privacy. Real client manuscripts are never shown without explicit, written consent.

Public Domain Demo: Dracula

Goal

Modernize a classic epistolary novel for a thriller audience.

Inputs

Public domain text (Project Gutenberg).

Process

Cut 15% (redundant journals). Added custom Gothic headers. Tightened scene transitions.

Result

A fast-paced 90k word thriller that retains the original voice but moves like a modern bestseller.

Synthetic Demo: "Crystal Tower"

Goal

Adapt a "System Apocalypse" LitRPG without overwhelming the reader with blue boxes.

Inputs

Synthetic web serial stream (generated text).

Process

Designed distinct "Interface" styles for stats. Consolidated repeated skill notifications into summary logs.

Result

High immersion. Stats enchance instead of interrupt the flow.

Synthetic Demo: "Heavenly Ascendant"

Goal

Fix "wandering protagonist" syndrome common in Xianxia serials.

Inputs

Synthetic cultivation story arc (120 chapters).

Process

Identified the "Tournament Arc" as the natural climax for Book 1. Cut 3 "side quest" chapters that killed pacing.

Result

A cohesive 140k word progression fantasy with a satisfying conclusion.
