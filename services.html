Common Questions

Do I keep my rights?

Yes. Even in the Royalty Share model, you retain full copyright. We sign a contract specifying the revenue split term or buyout options.

Do you use AI?

I use AI tools for initial analysis (pacing curves, typo hunting) to keep costs down, but every edit and creative decision is 100% human. You get the final say on all text.

What platforms do you support?

Deliverables are optimized for Amazon KDP (Kindle + Print). They also work for IngramSpark, Draft2Digital, and other wide aggregators.

How long does it take?

A typical 120k-word novel takes 4-6 weeks for the full process (Audit -> Edit -> Proof -> Format).
