The "Web Serial" Problem

Great stories, but can they be sold as books?

Pacing Issues

Serials are written chapter-by-chapter. Published novels need a cohesive arc, tighter pacing, and a satisfying climax.

Structural Bloat

Repetitive stats, recap chapters, and wandering subplots work on Royal Road but kill retention on Kindle.

Formatting Headaches

Copy-pasting from Google Docs can introduce hidden formatting issues that professional layout tools avoid.
